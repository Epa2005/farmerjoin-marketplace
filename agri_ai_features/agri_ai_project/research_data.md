# Rwanda Agricultural Research Data

## Common Crop Diseases and Remedies in Rwanda

| Crop | Disease (English) | Kinyarwanda Name | Symptoms | Remedies / Control Measures |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Banana** | Banana Xanthomonas Wilt (BXW) | Kirabiranya | Yellowing/wilting leaves, premature ripening, yellow ooze from cut stems. | Remove male buds, disinfect tools with fire or bleach, uproot infected plants. |
| **Banana** | Banana Bunchy Top Disease (BBTD) | - | Stunted growth, "bunched" leaves at top, dark green streaks on petioles. | Uproot and destroy infected mats, control aphids (vectors). |
| **Maize** | Maize Lethal Necrosis (MLN) | - | Severe mottling, leaf necrosis, stunted growth, sterile ears. | Crop rotation (avoid maize for 2 seasons), use certified seeds, control thrips/beetles. |
| **Maize** | Maize Streak Virus (MSV) | - | Linear yellow/white streaks along leaf veins. | Use resistant varieties, early planting, control leafhoppers. |
| **Maize** | Fall Armyworm (Pest) | Nkongwa y'ibigori | Ragged holes in leaves, sawdust-like frass in whorls. | Handpicking, use of neem extracts, timely pesticide application. |
| **Potato** | Late Blight | Imvura / Imvura y'ibirayi | Water-soaked spots on leaves, white mold on underside, tuber rot. | Use resistant varieties, fungicide application (Mancozeb/Ridomil), proper hilling. |
| **Potato** | Viral Diseases | Injungi | Leaf curling, yellowing, mosaic patterns. | Use certified clean seeds, control aphids. |
| **Cassava** | Cassava Mosaic Disease (CMD) | Ububembe | Mosaic pattern on leaves, leaf distortion, stunted growth. | Use resistant varieties, use clean cuttings, remove infected plants. |
| **Cassava** | Cassava Brown Streak Disease (CBSD) | - | Brown streaks on stems, yellowing of leaf veins, root necrosis (rot). | Use resistant varieties, early harvest, use clean planting material. |
| **Coffee** | Coffee Leaf Rust | Inshanyi | Orange powdery spots on leaf undersides. | Pruning for aeration, copper-based fungicides, resistant varieties. |
| **Coffee** | Coffee Berry Disease (CBD) | - | Dark sunken lesions on green berries, berry drop. | Fungicide application, proper pruning, resistant varieties. |
| **Rice** | Rice Blast | - | Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves, neck rot. | Balanced nitrogen use, resistant varieties, proper water management. |

## Rwanda Weather Patterns and Seasons

*   **Season A (Kiziba):** September to February. Short rainy season. Main crops: Beans, Maize, Irish Potatoes.
*   **Season B (Itumba):** March to June. Long rainy season. Main crops: Maize, Sorghum, Beans.
*   **Season C (Agasabyi):** July to August. Dry season. Focus on vegetables and sweet potatoes in marshlands/irrigated areas.

## Weather Prediction Factors for Rwanda
*   **Eastern Lowlands:** Warmer (20-21°C), drier.
*   **Bugarama Valley:** Warmest (23-24°C).
*   **Highlands (North/West):** Cooler, higher rainfall.
*   **Rainfall Fluctuations:** Patterns are shifting by 1-2 months due to climate change.
